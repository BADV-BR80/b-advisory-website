Cash flow app · Effective November 16, 2026

Privacy policy.

This policy covers the B Advisory Cash Flow application and its connection to QuickBooks Online.

Who we are

B Advisory provides financial advisory services to landscape companies and other small businesses. B Advisory Cash Flow is a tool we use to prepare weekly cash flow forecasts for the clients we advise. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app reads

With your authorization, the app reads the following from your QuickBooks Online company, through Intuit's API and with read-only access: company information; the chart of accounts; general ledger transactions for bank, credit card and operating line accounts; open and recently paid invoices and the customers they belong to; open bills and their vendors; purchases, deposits, transfers and journal entries that touch the bank accounts; and the balance sheet. It does not read payroll details, employee records, or anything outside the accounting data needed for a cash forecast, and it never writes to your books.

Why we read it

To produce your rolling 13-week cash flow forecast and weekly cash plan, to measure each week's actual cash movement against the forecast, and to advise you. We do not use your data for any other purpose, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

How the connection works

You connect once through Intuit's own sign-in and consent screen (OAuth 2.0). We never see or store your QuickBooks password. Intuit issues the app a token that can be revoked at any time by you or by us. Tokens are stored encrypted; the app's credentials are held only in the runtime environment, never in code or documents.

Where the data is kept, and for how long

Data is processed and stored in a private cloud environment operated by Anthropic in the United States, in workspaces accessible only to B Advisory. Forecasts are delivered to you on a private web page and by email through Google Workspace. We keep your ledger data and forecasts for the duration of our engagement. When you disconnect the app or the engagement ends, the token is revoked and your data is deleted within 30 days, unless you ask us to retain a final copy of your forecasts.

Who can see it

Your data and forecasts are visible to you and to your B Advisory advisor. We do not share them with third parties, other than the service providers above that host and transmit them on our behalf, or where the law requires it. Each client's data is kept separate from every other client's.

Security

Access is limited to B Advisory accounts protected by multi-factor authentication. Credentials and tokens are never stored in plain text in shared files. Every API call is logged with Intuit's transaction identifier so problems can be traced. If we became aware of a breach affecting your data, we would notify you promptly.

Your choices

You can disconnect the app at any time (see how to disconnect), ask what data we hold about your company, or ask us to delete it, by emailing brady@b-advisory.net.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
