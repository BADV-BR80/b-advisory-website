Cash flow app

A weekly 13-week cash plan, built from your books.

B Advisory Cash Flow is a private tool B Advisory uses to prepare weekly cash flow forecasts for the businesses it advises, from their QuickBooks Online data.

What it does

Every Monday the app reads the client's bank and credit card ledgers, open invoices and bills, and balance sheet from QuickBooks Online, measures how last week went against the forecast, and produces a 13-week cash plan: what must be paid, what can wait, what to collect, and whether the operating line needs to move.

How it connects

A client authorizes the connection once, through Intuit's standard sign-in and consent screen, using their own QuickBooks Online login. The app has read-only access to accounting data and never writes to the client's books. The connection can be removed at any time from QuickBooks or by asking B Advisory; see how to disconnect.

Who sees the results

Each client's forecast is delivered to that client and to their B Advisory advisor on a private page and by email. No client's data is visible to another client.
