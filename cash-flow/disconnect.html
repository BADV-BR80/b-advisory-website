Disconnecting from QuickBooks Online.

You can remove the app's access to your QuickBooks Online company at any time. It takes effect immediately.

From QuickBooks Online

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as the company administrator.
  2. Open Apps in the left menu, then My apps.
  3. Find B Advisory Cash Flow and choose Disconnect.

Or ask us

Email brady@b-advisory.net from the address on file for your company and we will revoke the connection on our side the same business day.

What happens next

  • The app's access token is revoked at Intuit and deleted from our systems; no further data is read from your company.
  • Data already pulled for your forecasts is deleted within 30 days of disconnecting, unless you ask us to keep a final copy of your forecasts.
  • Reconnecting later is the same one-time authorization through Intuit's consent screen.
